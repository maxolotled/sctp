Confirm expensive buys

MOD

Clicking an item in someone else's shop chest buys it straight away. For pricey shops, the mod now asks you to click twice.

  • When a shop's price is at or above your limit (32 DB to start with), the first click only outlines the item in amber with “Click again to buy” and the price.
  • Click the same item again within 4 seconds to buy it. Cheaper shops and your own shops work like before.
  • Turn it off or change the limit in Settings › Alerts.

2

The item page for what you're holding

MOD

Wondering what something in your hand is worth? Hold X and press I.

  • It opens that item's page: every listing for it and its price history. Rares open their rare page with all their details.
  • In your inventory or a chest, hover over an item and press X + I to open the page for that item instead.
  • X on its own still opens the dashboard, now when you let go of it.
  • Both keys can be changed in Options › Controls › Shop Logger.
Keys X + I
3

Sorting that follows your search

MOD & WEBSITE

Browsing shows what's new; searching shows what's cheapest. And your filters stay put.

  • With nothing typed you see the most recent listings first. Type something and it switches to cheapest first (then most recent).
  • Pick a sort yourself and that's what you get from then on, until you reset the filters.
  • Your world, type and other filters are remembered: on the site in your browser, and in the mod for the Listings, Marketplace, item and seller screens.
  • Item pages always list the cheapest first, and the marketplace got a Newest / Price switch.
4

Find rares by base item

MOD & WEBSITE

Looking for a fancy elytra or a special sword? Search the rare library for the item it's made from.

  • Searching elytra or netherite sword also finds the rares made from that item, in the item library and in-game.
  • The base item comes from what the mod sees in shops, so it covers every rare that's been for sale.
5

Sell your bugs at Sefi

MOD

Caught a pile of bugs in your Butterfly Garden? Selling them at the Creepy Crawly Exchange at spawn is one click now. Thanks to ZyskiDev!

  • Open the Creepy Crawly Exchange and press the new Sell button: the mod sells your bugs for you, one at a time. Press it again to stop.
  • The bugs you can sell are highlighted, so you can see what's going in.
  • Carrying a bug that belongs to someone else? Selling is blocked until you put it away, so you never sell another player's bug by accident.
6

Credit the original artist

WEBSITE

Made a map from someone else's artwork? Give them credit.

  • On your mapart, fill in Original art by when you upload a map or edit one.
  • It shows as “original art by …” on the mapart page and its card, under who built it.
