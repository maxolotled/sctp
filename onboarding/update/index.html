All-new mod menus

MOD

Every Shop Logger screen was redesigned to look and feel like the website, and it's much easier to get around. Press X in-game to open it. Every feature from before is still there.

  • A dashboard home: your world, scanner and watchlist at a glance, a search box that jumps straight to the listings, and a tile for every section.
  • Tabs on every screen to jump between Listings, Items, Rares, Watchlist, Marketplace, Mapart and Settings without backing out through menus.
  • One Settings screen with categories and a short explanation under every option (Advanced settings is folded in). If a line gets cut off on a small window, hover it to read the whole thing.
  • Item pages with tabs for current listings, price history and rare details.

Easier filters

MOD

Filters no longer make you click through a long list one option at a time.

  • Click any filter to pick from a list. Long lists (categories, months) get a search box too. Shift+click still steps to the next option.
  • Rares: filter by release date instead of dyeable. Pick a year or a single month, oldest first.
  • Your place is kept: open a rare or item, go back, and the list is scrolled right where you left it.

Watch items from their page

MOD

Looking at a rare or item and want an alert when it's listed? No more going back to the Watchlist to search for it.

  • Every item page has a + Add to watchlist button next to its tabs. It adds the item and opens its watchlist options (max price, skip display/no-price listings) right there.
  • Already watching it? The button says Watchlist settings and takes you straight to its options.
4

In-game marketplace filters

MOD

The in-game Marketplace screen can now narrow down posts.

  • World: Firefly, Honeybee or both. It starts on the world you're in; cross-world posts show under either.
  • Show: all posts, only Selling, or only Looking for.
5

Handy extras

MOD
  • Ender chest ⇄ backpack: arrows beside your ender chest and backpack screens. Left opens your backpack (/bp), right your ender chest (/ec). Don't want them? Turn them off in Settings.
  • Glowing chest outline: a new teleport highlight style that outlines the shop's chest instead of drawing a beam across your screen. Settings › Highlights & tools › Teleport beam style.
  • Always the current price: scans re-read the shop sign every time, so a lowered price shows up right away in watchlist alerts and on the site.
  • Dashboard tiles no longer cut their names short when a badge like "Scanning off" is showing.

Mapart likes

WEBSITE

Show some appreciation: every mapart now has a heart. Liking needs an account, one like per piece.

  • Like from the mapart catalog, a mapart's own page, or the mapart of the day on the home page.
  • Sort the catalog by Most liked to see the community's favourites.

Marketplace on the site

WEBSITE
  • Written job reviews: besides 👍 / 👎, write how it went (up to 500 characters). Reviews show on the job, and you can edit or remove your own.
  • Renew your posts: a "Renew 14 days" button under your own listings and job posts in Mine pushes the expiry date out, even right after a post expired. Each post shows when it expires.
  • Fixed Mine sometimes saying you had to log in while you were already logged in.

Mapart scanner is off for now

Mapart uploads are paused while we fix a few things, so the in-game scanner now starts switched off for everyone. You can still open the Mapart screen and turn it on yourself.

Good to know: enabling this feature will capture all maps in item frames visible to you.
