My storage

MOD

Never dig through twenty chests for one stack again. Add your chests once, and the mod remembers what's in each of them, and where.

  • Look at a chest, barrel or shulker box and press X + M to add it. Press it again to take it back out.
  • Look at anything else and press X + M to add every container within 16 blocks at once.
  • Each container is read the first time you're close enough to open it. After that it updates whenever you open it yourself, and keeps a short log of what you put in and took out.
  • Shulker boxes inside your chests count too, and your ender chest is kept up to date every time you open it.
  • It's all kept on your own computer. Nothing about your storage is uploaded.
  • All the mod's keys are now changed in Settings › Keys instead of Options › Controls. Keys you'd changed are carried over.
Shop chests stay out. Your storage is for your own containers; shops keep being scanned for the Trading Post as always.
Keys X + M looking at a chest: add it · looking at anything else: add all nearby

Find what you have

MOD

Where did you put those diamonds? Ask the mod, and every chest holding them lights up.

  • The new My storage tab lists everything you own across your chests, with a search box. Click an item to find it.
  • Every container holding it lights up with a white glow you can see through walls, and the line above your hotbar shows the nearest one's coordinates. It goes away when you open one of them, or press X.
  • Already have something you're shopping for? Items in your storage get a chest icon in item search and on shop listings, and a banner on their item page. Click it to find yours first.
  • Sort by most, fewest, A to Z or what changed lately, and narrow it down: only rares, only vanilla items, a rare category or type (all your snails, all your Decor), or a creative menu group like Redstone.
  • Mark anything with a ★ ◆ ❤ ● ▲ or ■ (the ☆ on its row), then show just that mark: borrowed items, favourite tools, whatever you keep losing.
  • Opening one lit-up container turns off just that one, and inside it the slots holding the item are outlined. Find 5 unread lights up the nearest containers still waiting for their first read.
  • Bundles count too, and the Containers view shows each chest's contents and what changed lately.

Storage holograms

MOD

See what's in a chest without opening it.

  • Containers in your storage show what's inside on the lower half of every side that's open to air: the main item and how many.
  • Colours and wood types count together (all your wool is just Wool). A chest without one clear main item says Mixed and how many items it holds.
  • Turn them off, or pick a beacon beam instead of the glow, in Settings › My storage. That's also where you'll find every container you've added.

Notepad

MOD & WEBSITE

A shopping list that ticks itself off. Press N.

  • Add what you need and how many. As the items arrive in your inventory, each line counts down, and it's ticked off once you have them all.
  • TP on a line takes you to the cheapest shop selling it right now.
  • Building something? On the build list, press Copy for the notepad and paste it in the mod. Or take what's still missing from Litematica's material list.
  • A Notes page for everything else: “holding Snaruto for Tina”, “restock glass”.
  • Either page can stay on screen as a small card; drag it wherever you like.

Tidier chat

MOD

Selling a stack of butterflies used to fill your whole chat. Not anymore.

  • When the exact same message shows up again within 2 minutes, the earlier one disappears and the new one gets ×2, ×3 and so on.
  • It works even when other messages came in between.
  • A message with a linked item, like two different shulkers both called [Shulker Box], gets a ▸: click it to see every message again, each with its own item.
  • On by default. Turn it off in Settings › Look & tools.
In chat You sold a Monarch Butterfly for 2 coins ×5

Prices from real sales

MOD & WEBSITE

What something is listed for and what it actually sells for aren't the same. SCTP now leans on the sales.

  • Your purchases count: when you buy from a shop, the mod checks that chest again right away and reports the sale within seconds.
  • More sales spotted: a little stock disappearing from a shop that's still well stocked counts as sold too. A chest that's emptied or cleared out doesn't.
  • Values follow sales: the price check on rare pages, and other values across the site, lean on what items sold for in the last 90 days. Not enough sales yet? Then it's 75% of the average listing price, since asking prices run high.
  • Every value says what it's based on, like “22 sold and 5 listings”. And every “sold” number on the site counts these sales.

Build list upgrades

WEBSITE

The build list turns a Litematica material list into a shopping route. Now it also tells you when crafting is cheaper.

  • When buying the ingredients and crafting them (or using a stonecutter) costs at least half less than buying the block, or nobody sells the block at all, the list buys the ingredients instead. Planks from logs, glass panes from glass, stairs on a stonecutter.
  • Prefer the real thing? Press Buy the block instead on any of them.
  • Tick off what you've bought: every line has a checkbox that crosses it out, and a shop dims once you're done there. Your ticks stay when you reload.

Wishlist stars everywhere

WEBSITE

Star the rares and mapart you want, right where you find them.

  • Every card in the rare library and the mapart catalog has a ☆ in its corner when you're logged in. Click it to add the item to your wishlist, click again to take it off.
  • It's the same wishlist as on your collection, per world.

More on the site

WEBSITE
  • Sort every list: the rare and vanilla libraries, the mapart catalog and your collection can be sorted A to Z, Z to A, newest or oldest (and mapart by likes or size). Your choice is remembered.
  • Mapart by size: filter the catalog by dimensions, like 2×2 or 4×3.
  • Rares by base item: rare pages now show the item a rare is made from, with a link to every rare made from it, and the rare library has an exact Base item filter.
  • Notifications: a small badge next to your name shows when you have unread notifications.
  • Fairer averages: a seller's average price leaves out listings priced over 10 times the usual price, so one silly price no longer skews it.
