Rare-dle in-game

MOD

Play today's Rare-dle without leaving the game. Press X and open the new Rare-dle tile.

  • Same game as the website: the same daily rare, and your guesses count toward the same streak, points and leaderboard.
  • No password: the mod signs you in with your Minecraft name, the same way a server checks it when you join. It plays on the sctp.nl account with that name verified, so verify your name on the site first.
  • Type a rare's name and press Enter (or click it). Each guess shows how close it is, the pixel hint sharpens as you go, and the answer shows when you're done.

Empty hand for doors, chests and more

MOD

Some items do something whenever you right-click with them, even when you only wanted to open a door. Now you can tell the mod to click with an empty hand instead.

  • Go to Settings › Empty hand, press Add, hold the item and press X. Add as many items as you like.
  • From then on, clicking doors, chests, buttons, levers, crafting tables and every other clickable block uses an empty slot (or a plain vanilla item), so your item doesn't go off. Works everywhere, not just in shop worlds.
  • Items match exactly: adding your named sword leaves your other swords alone.
  • Want to use the item on a block after all? Sneak while you click.

Double chest shops

MOD

Double chests are scanned as shops now, as one shop for both halves.

  • The sign on the left half (as you face the chest) is the price for everything inside.
  • If only the right half has a sign, that one counts.
4

Highlights and beams

MOD

The mod can now outline a chest the way the game does when you look at a block, only brighter, in colour, and visible through walls.

  • Something sold? Your shop chest with a payment waiting gets a green outline you can spot from across your shop, instead of a green particle.
  • New teleport styles (Settings › Look & tools › Teleport beam style): Chest highlight (through walls) outlines the shop you're heading to, and Beacon beam at the chest puts a real beacon beam on it.
  • Stock holograms (off by default, Settings › Look & tools): a small “64 in stock · 3m ago” line on the front of scanned shop chests, printed flat under the sign. It only shows when you’re close and in front of the chest.
  • Empty shops you just walked past get a grey marker on their sign instead of red, so you can skip them.

Handy extras

MOD
  • Ender chest ⇄ backpack is one button now, on the left: it shows the one you're not in (a bundle for your backpack, an ender chest for /ec).
  • Hide display listings: the Display chip on the Listings screen (or Settings › Look & tools) leaves [DISPLAY] shops out of listings, item pages, seller profiles and chat /search.

Rare auctions

WEBSITE

Sell your rares at an SCTP auction. When one is coming up, the homepage shows it with the date, host, drop-off and spots left.

  • On sctp.nl/auction, press the big button and search your rares: each one lands in a table with SCTP's value and suggested prices already filled in.
  • Dutch clock auctions start high and the price keeps dropping until someone claims the item; regular auctions go up from a starting price. Not sure what to ask? Tick Let the host pick.
  • You get your shulker name (Registered <you>) and where to drop it off. My Auctions shows what sold and what you receive.
7

Wishlists

WEBSITE

Star (☆) the rares and mapart you want on My Collection, per world.

  • Once you tick an item as owned, its star goes away by itself.
  • Share it: the Wishlist card has a Copy wishlist link button, and that link opens your public collection straight on your wishlist.
  • Filter the checklist to Wishlist, or make a wishlist picture with "Share what I still need".

More on the site

WEBSITE
  • Shop Logger is on Modrinth: install and update it straight from the Modrinth app. CurseForge and the direct download still work.
  • Every language: item names from a game set to German, French or any other language are turned back into English names, so they show up in searches like everyone else's.
  • Real sales: “sold” on item pages and the statistics pages now only counts real sales, when a shop’s stock went down and its payment showed up in the chest. Restocking or taking items out no longer counts.
  • 20 new rares from the item sheet, plus updated details for many others.
