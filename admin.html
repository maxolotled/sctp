FAQ (shown on docs.html)

Blocked sellers

Blocking removes their listings immediately and stops any future upload from them showing up — permanent until unblocked.

Rare item approvals

Rare-catalog items priced at exactly 1 or 2 diamond are held here instead of showing up publicly, since that's suspiciously cheap for a rare — approve or deny each one.

Manually add listings

No commas inside a field. priceLabel is optional — defaults to "price currency". Each entry gets a unique id (M001, M002, ...) shown in the Last seen column on the site instead of a timestamp.

Delete an entire shop

Removes every listing for a seller — for a shop that's gone entirely (player quit, moved, torn down), not for clearing one stale item (use a report or the manual-delete box above for that).
