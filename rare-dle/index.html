How to play

Rares originating from another rare or chance box/similar are never the secret rare of the day (all plushies etc, for example)

There's one secret rare item each day (same for everyone, changes at midnight UTC). Guess any rare — each guess shows how it compares to the secret one:

  • Category, type/slot, dyeable, particles: green when they match. Type/slot and particles turn orange when they only partly match.
  • Released: green for the same date, orange when it's within about 3 months (or the same year), labeled "more recent" or "older" to tell you which way the secret rare's date differs.
  • Obtained from: green for the same source, orange when it's the same kind (e.g. another crate).
  • Pixel hint: after 4 guesses you see the rare's icon as a 2×2 picture, then 3×3 after 5, 4×4 after 6 and 8×8 after 7.

Scoring: fewer guesses = more points (1000 down to 120), plus a bonus of 25 points per day of win streak (up to 10 days). A missed day or a loss resets your streak. No-peek bonus: +125 points if you don't open the Rare Items pages (the rare list or a rare's detail page) while your game is in progress.
