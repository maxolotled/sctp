# Frequently Asked Questions
::: details How do I install the mod?
Please check out the [installation](./installation) page.
:::
::: details How does the mod work?
The mod scans all shop chests you are within reach of, and uploads this data to the SCTP website via an API. 
::: details What are the yellow inventory slots for?
They're meant to mark items available at /pw RentARare (if currently playing on Firefly) or /pw tool_library (if currently playing on Honeybee)!
:::
::: details Shops I visit aren't being updated/deleted
- Items are uploaded every 15 minutes, and may take 15 minutes to show up on the website/database.
- If they still don't show up, try uploading manually via the settings menu (press X) and check the output in chat.
- Still not solved? Reach out!
:::
::: details Why are my chests opening/closing?! Am I being followed by ghosts?
No worries! It's expected behavior; the mod has to open all chests to check their contents, but you won't see the inventory screen show up.
:::
::: details Can I enter stuff manually, for example stuff I sell at a PW?
Yes! Please DM me on discord, so I can give you more information for that.
:::
