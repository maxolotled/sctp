    The vault and the answers

    The vault's block decides the three map squares (whorls I–III). The key (entered in the mod) and the word on the sign in the house (first place only) are stored hashed: type a new one to replace it.

    The Apex

    Opens when a hunter solves Whorl IV's four chapters; it points to the island. Any x z inside the island box below solves it. After it, the arrows (Whorl IV's key) are entered in the mod. Marks are drawn on the world map for them only.

    Hunters who apply get their personal key (note secret:job) as a notification. Saved with the Apex.

    Finishing places

    The order hunters enter the arrows in the mod. For their place the mod marks a block in gold (outline and beam, no coordinates) and shows the password, which they name a paper with and drop into the hopper there. One line per place or range; the narrowest range that fits wins.

    One per line: from to x y z password (the password may have spaces). Example: 2 5 … covers places 2 to 5.

    Chapters

    Solves, wrong answers and hints taken per chapter. Hints only come out when you release them here (for everyone at once); set one back to “Nothing” to take it back.

    Broadcast

    A one-off message to every hunter: a toast in the mod and a banner on /hunt/.

    Shared content

    Chapters (id, whorl, ord, title, clue, hints [nudge, push, shove], fragment) and whorls (whorl, title, intro, puzzle, nudge), as JSON. The same for every hunter.

    Doors

    Hidden pages on sctp.nl (sctp.nl/<slug>). Each hunter's door text comes from their puzzle set (note context door:<slug>). Times are each hunter's own local time; while the hunt is off (testers only) doors are always open.

    Puzzle sets

    Personal puzzles from scripts/hunt/generate.js: upload its sets.json (sent 25 at a time), then its pictures. Every hunter gets one set at sign-up.
