Statement

Snailcraft Trading Post & the SCTP mod

A statement from SCTP

  • Accurate, live pricing
  • Watchlist notifications
  • Marketplace

As you know, some features (accurate, live pricing - watchlist notifications - marketplace) were removed from the website and the most recent mod version, meaning they can no longer be used.

This decision wasn't made by us.

The admins say they have received complaints about the mod, website and it's functions and decided it would be best for the server to have these features removed; they also claim the features make reselling too efficient and promote the use of 3rd party websites instead of the Discord trade channels.

The SCTP team has not seen any decline in usage of the trade channels (everyone uploading to the marketplace is still posting to trade chats), and the website was designed around preventing reselling/overcharging. We have provided these arguments as well as live usage statistics (3k+ website users, 250+ mod users) to the admin team. The Snailcraft admins/owner have required us to remove these features as soon as possible, and we were left with no choice but to comply;

  • No community feedback was considered, and all feedback from mod/site users was rejected;
  • These features were approved by the admin team before being added, and are now being reconsidered, seemingly out of nowhere;
  • We were not allowed to discuss these changes in chats prior to them being made or gather feedback ourselves.

We hope you will continue to use the website and mod, even though these serious changes in the functions of it; thank you for your support! If you have any questions or would like to know more, you can reach out to us via Discord. However, we direct you to the SC admins/owner for any complaints.
